## What changed that matters

**AutoDuty**

- **Beast tier lists:**
  - New settings `Meta.Crucible.BeastTierLists` (a list, so overrides cannot set it) and `BeastTierListIndex`.
  - With the default list, Recommended mode picks the same beasts as before.
- **BossMod settings are temporary now:**
  - AutoDuty applies its BossMod settings once, inside `Run`, and restores the player's values when it stops. These are
    PreventMovingWhileCasting=true, GTMode, DashSafety, AllowAutomaticActions and AllowAutomaticInteract.
  - It does this only while `AutoManageBossModAISettings` is on.
  - Crucible SUX can therefore set `ActionTweaksConfig.PreventMovingWhileCasting` back to false right after `Run`
    returns, through `BossMod.Configuration`. That is what lets the original BossMod dodge while casting. AutoDuty does
    not apply the setting again during the run.
- **Unchanged:** item use, rest and the campsite beast-HP bug (the HP/HP comparison).
  - AutoDuty heals with items only from the player's HP: at or below 40% in a battle, at or below 60% on the board.
    It never checks a beast's HP.
  - While a familiar covers the player after Snarl, the player's HP barely drops. AutoDuty then uses no healing item at
    all, even while the beasts are being knocked out.

**Both BossMods** still register the same `BossMod.*` IPC names. Reloading the kept one after the other unloads is still
needed.

- The original BossMod's `xan.CrucibleAI` now uses Challenge on `PreferProvoking` hints. Those come from the Mindflayer
  and Corpse Flower modules.
- BossMod Reborn has had no Crucible changes since 2026-10-09.
- BossMod Reborn's Beastmaster rotation defines Snarl as 46571, which is "Impact Zone". The right id is 46751. It
  matters only when BossMod Reborn is the rotation.

**Rotation Solver Reborn's Beastmaster rotation** uses Snarl and Challenge:

- With its defaults, only one Snarl: on a target with status 2552, a Board 1 mechanic.
- An off-by-default "FamiliarTankSwap" option.
- It uses no items.

**FFXIVClientStructs** (in Dalamud 15.0.3.8) has `InstanceContentCrucibleOfTheUnbroken`:

- the 10-slot Crucible inventory (`InventorySlot.ItemId`, an XBMItem row)
- `UseItem(slot, beastId)`, which acts on the current target. `beastId` is used only by Blessed Horn.

Builds need that Dalamud. CI and the release workflow already download the latest one.

## Beastmaster aggro facts

- **Snarl** (46751): the familiar uses Threaten (46752), which gives it top enmity for 15 s. Cover lasts up to 45 s.
  Snarl also removes the player's Enmity Up.
- **Challenge** (46750): gives the player top enmity for 30 s and removes the familiar's Cover.
- Both share cooldown group 81 (15 s recast) and have a 25 y range.
- **Statuses:** Cover is 2412, on the familiar. Covered is 2413, on the player.
- **Only one familiar is out at a time:** the gauge has a single `ActiveBattlehorn`.
