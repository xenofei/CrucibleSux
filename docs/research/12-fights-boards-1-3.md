## Where the tester's fights are
- "Ogre piece" = Board 1, Move 6, ELITE node: Ogre Piece + Wisp Piece + Great Wisp Piece [B1] (2+ with the Ogre Piece page). The AoE the tester got hit by is almost certainly Scorching Smite (cone), Allfire (scattered circles), Lava Puddle, or Fire Call chase fireball.
- "Pas de seul" = Board 1, Move 9, BOSS: Pas de Seul (succubus) + Succubus Mage (north) + 2 Succubus Knights (south) [B1] (2+ with Pas de Seul / Succubus Mage pages).
- Guttler the Gutter and Thanatos Piece = Board 3, Move 12 boss [B3, SB] (2+).
- Lauda the Spellcleaver = Second MASTER's Board boss, not Boards 1-3 (Lauda page). Toxic Mass = First MASTER's Board (Toxic Mass page). Borgny (Borgny the Venomous) = not found; no wiki page (404). Only an NPC line in the quest Free for All calls him the "final challenger" of a new board; Second Master's Board is a guess (inf, unverified).
- Note: the Guttler page lists Beastly Aura (Wind, universal) and Thunderbolt (Lightning, front AoE, Paralysis); the Lauda page lists the identical two actions. Treat as template overlap; SB gives fuller Guttler detail.

## Cross-board facts
- Campsite Boards 1-2: player alone 90% max HP; +1 familiar 45% each; +2 familiars 30% each [B1,B2] (2+). Board 3: 100% / 60% / 40% / 30% (1 to 3 familiars) [B3].
- No natural regen for player or beasts; beast at 0 HP is out until revived at campsite or by item; player at 0 HP fails the run. Potions are disabled, food works [PU] (1; web search snippet of Live Letter coverage agrees on no regen).
- Only 3 familiars per battle, one active at a time via Battlehorns [PU]. PU advises Snarl so sturdy familiars tank, 3-pet teams for Legendary, bring lots of food (1).
- Many AoEs apply Damage Down / Vulnerability Up; both are damage taxes, so avoid every telegraph.
- Weakness lists below are what the board's Board Layout shows. Status "vulnerabilities" = statuses that stick well (Slow, Stun, Poison etc.).

# BOARD 1 (Lv30, rank 1, 9 moves, 10 roster) [B1]

Route: M1 Bone Knight+Bishop; M2 split (L Arch Demon / R Piscodemon); M3 split (L Campsite #1 / R Treasure #1); M4 Banemite+Miteling; M5 Treasure #2; M6 ELITE Ogre+Wisps; M7 split (L Treasure #3 / R Campsite #2); M8 Shop; M9 Boss Pas de Seul. Both campsites are after splits so one path skips each. Campsite #2 (M7 right) is right before shop and boss; treasure #3 lies on the opposite branch. Treasure picks 4 of ~10 (Reraisers G1/G2, Crucible Ash, Beast Potion Kit among them). Shop M8 (Territory Tokens): choose 8 simulars, tonic/hydrolixer/primafodder, 4 from each of two groups (Crucible Antidote, G1/G2 Beast Potion, Tome of the Impervious, Vampiric Essence; armor). [B1] (1 each).

## M1 Bone Knight + Bone Bishop (normal) [B1] (1)
- Weak: Blunt. Both vuln Heavy; Knight also Slow, Interruption.
- Bone Knight: Tumulus = 5y circle, Damage Down (step out). Ossify = INTERRUPT (Physical Damage Up). Skullsplinter = tankbuster (let a sturdy beast take it / Snarl). Forward Guard = blocks frontal player damage until broken, ignores beasts (attack from side/behind or let beasts hit it).
- Bone Bishop: Death Spiral = donut (stand inside hole or far out), Damage Down. Black Eruption = circle that splits into four, Vulnerability Up (move off the origin and the four splits). Ancient Aero = line, Windburn (cleanse).
- Kill priority (inf): Bishop (squishier caster with the nastier AoEs), interrupt Ossify on Knight.

## M2L Arch Demon Piece (normal) [B1] (1)
- Weak: Lightning; vuln Poison.
- Abyssal Swing = frontal cone. Abyssal Charge = line AoEs. Dismember = five line AoEs across arena. Abyssal Transfixion = circles around arena, then three on the player.
- Single enemy; dodge lines/circles; use lightning beasts.

## M2R Piscodemon Piece (normal) [B1] (1)
- Weak: Slashing; vuln Heavy, Poison.
- Void Blizzard III = moving AoEs. Clear Mind (removable Damage Up) then Arcane Blast = remove the buff/avoid blast. Void Flare Star = proximity AoE (stay away from boss at that cast). Void Thunder III = cross of lines.

## M4 Banemite Piece + Miteling Piece (normal) [B1] (1)
- Weak: Ice.
- Banemite: Bedrock Uplift = circle then donut waves. Deadly Thrust = tankbuster, Poison. Venom Web = center circle then eight edge AoEs.
- Miteling: Silkscreen = line AoE, Heavy. Kill order (inf): Miteling is a weak add; Banemite is the threat; decide by whether Heavy matters.

## M6 ELITE: Ogre Piece + Wisp Piece + Great Wisp Piece [B1 + Ogre_Piece page] (2+)
- Ogre Lv30 Voidsent; Weak: Wind (and Water for group per B1); vuln Blind, Poison (Ogre page). STR 4, PhysRes 4, CON 4, INT 3, MagRes 2 stars.
- Scorching Smite (Fire, front AoE) = cone from a cardinal edge; Ogre dives into lava puddles. Not interruptible. Move out of cone.
- Allfire = scattered circles. Lava Puddle = Burns if stood in. Burning Ward = Wisps home in on you; Great Wisps have more HP and hit harder. Fire Call = chase tether, fireball (run it away from others/out of puddles). Straight Punch (Fire) = tankbuster (single target).
- Kill order (inf, not stated by any source): Great Wisps/Wisps when they home in, since they are the pressure add; Ogre is the damage sponge. Use wind/water beasts and Blind/Poison.
- Danger: elite; main source of unavoidable chip plus puddle Burns. Tester's AoE hit most likely cone/Allfire/puddle.

## M9 BOSS: Pas de Seul + Succubus Mage + Succubus Knights [B1, Pas_de_Seul, Succubus_Mage pages] (2+)
- Pas de Seul Lv30 aggressive Voidsent. Weak: Piercing, Fire (B1; boss page lists Piercing). No vulnerabilities. STR 2, PhysRes 3, CON 5, INT 5, MagRes 5 stars (very tanky vs magic).
- Blood Rain = circle or donut, HEALS her. Blood Sword = tankbuster, heals her. Cold Caress = tankbuster on highest enmity, Poison (cleanse). Void Aero II = line AoE then seven cones. Beguiling Mist = circle, Nightmare. Heart tether = detonates, Seduced (break/avoid tether). Summon = Succubus Mage north + two Succubus Knights south.
- Succubus Mage (Lv30): Fire weak; vuln Slow, Interruption, Flat damage; STR1 PhysRes1 CON2 INT2 MagRes1 (squishy). Aero. Fanatacism/Fanaticism = single-target ally buff, Damage Up, INTERRUPTIBLE and nullifiable. Void Fire II = ground circle AoE (Fire) on highest enmity target, Vulnerability Up; not interruptible. Dodge by moving off after the marker.
- Succubus Knight: Sweet Steel = frontal cone, Vulnerability Up (stay out of front).
- Kill priority (inf): Mage first (squishiest, buffs the boss/knights via Fanaticism and throws Void Fire II); interrupt Fanaticism; then Knights; boss last. Mage stand N, knights S: send a beast/ranged damage north. Blood Rain/Blood Sword heal her, so burst after the dodge/tankbuster window.
- Piercing/fire beasts; Slow and Interruption on Mage.

# BOARD 2 (Lv40, rank 6, 10 moves, 12 roster) [B2]

Route: M1 Manticore; M2 Shop #1 (guaranteed Lily Simular); M3 split (L ELITE Taurus+Ahrimans / R Wyvern); M4 split (L Treasure #1 / R Campsite #1); M5 Voidmancer+Zombies; M6 Treasure #2; M7 ELITE Elder+Younger Tablitaur; M8 split (L random Demon / Evil Weapon / Devilet fight or campsite, R Treasure #3); M9 Shop #2; M10 Boss Loosefrox Inkyjots + Chewchum Popoto. Taking M3R and M4R skips the elite and gets the campsite. [B2] (1 for all items below unless noted.)

## M1 Manticore Piece
- Weak: Wind; vuln Poison.
- Arm and Hammer = 180 degree AoE on glowing-arm side, Damage Down. Deadly Hold = tankbuster. Hammerleap = jumps to a random arena edge, large static circle, Vulnerability Up. Heads and Tails = two 180 degree AoEs (head and tail). Charge and Hammer = four telegraphed static line charges plus Arm and Hammer from each arm.

## M3L ELITE Taurus Piece + Ahriman Pieces
- Taurus weak: Piercing, Ice; vuln Slow, Paralysis, Blind, Stun, Sleep.
- Mortal Ray = unavoidable damage + uncleansable Doom 25s; remove Doom by standing on one of four stone tiles for 10s. Ruinous Ring = donut centered on Taurus; Ruinous Locus = circle centered on Taurus; Contraction = ring then locus; Expansion = locus then ring (Vulnerability Up). Ray of Ignorance = marks a player, drops Aetheric Charge pulsing expanding circles (high magic dmg, Damage Down). Aetherial Fissure = voidgates fire static lines across arena. Summon = one Ahriman in each corner.
- Ahriman: Stone auto; Mortal Gaze = gaze (shrinking ring), uncleansable Doom 25s; Eyes on Me = self-destruct, unavoidable.
- Priority (inf): kill Ahrimans (they cause Doom/explode) or ignore; manage Doom tiles.

## M3R Wyvern Piece
- Weak: Lightning; vuln Blind, Poison.
- Storm's Grip = damage wall on perimeter + two Wind Sprites (N/S) casting Buffet (large line, Vuln Up). Typhoon = radial knockback, whirlwinds drift to far wall (Damage Down + knock-up). Liquid Hell = circles become lava, Burns 15s. Blazing Trail = Wyvern flies to a side, 180 degree cone. Storm Trail = three 60 degree cones.

## M5 Voidmancer Piece + Zombie Pieces
- Voidmancer weak: Earth, Ice; vuln Slow, Petrification, Bind, Heavy, Flat damage.
- Death Drive = ring on a player then circle 4s later; revives zombie corpses inside. Dark Orb = large circle centered on caster. Evil Mist = unavoidable; green orbs pulse circles; each orb hit adds Wit's End.
- Zombie: each hit adds Wit's End; 16 stacks = Confused 12s (attack own beast). Kill Voidmancer first (inf) since it revives zombies and feeds Wit's End; keep zombies off the player (Snarl/beast tank).

## M7 ELITE Elder Tablitaur + Younger Tablitaur
- Elder weak: Fire; vuln Interruption, Blind, Heavy, Poison.
- Elder: 100-tonze Slash = line tankbuster paired with Rallying Cheer. Endless Swipes = 60 degree cone + rotating arrow.
- Younger: Rallying Cheer = INTERRUPT (else Elder gets Damage Up, removable 15s). Endless Swing = chase tether; Younger becomes untargetable and spins at you (dmg+knockback).
- Both: 1000-tonze Swipe = 180 degree from both fronts; 1111-tonze Swing = massive circle; 10-tonze Stomp = knockbacks at adjacent corners. When one dies the survivor gains permanent Damage Up stack (Disorienting Groan) and Endless Slashes = eight unavoidable hits to player and beast (Cover doubles beast's hits). Kill them close together/Younger first to stop Rallying Cheer (inf).

## M8L Demon / Evil Weapon / Devilet (random node, or campsite)
- Demon weak: Slashing, Ice, Earth (listed for the set). Void Paralyze = unavoidable + Paralysis 30s (cleanse). Condemnation = 90 degree frontal cone.
- Evil Weapon: Smite of Excessive Rage = sword spins horizontal -> donut; vertical -> lines front and back.
- Devilet: Void Fire III long cast unavoidable on player (priority kill/interrupt candidate, inf).
- Aetheric Charge (also listed): Recharge feeds a random enemy aether; at 5 stacks permanent Damage Up. Dark Eruption = four consecutive circles under player.

## M10 BOSS: Loosefrox Inkyjots (Blunt weak; vuln Blind, Poison) + Chewchum Popoto (Water weak)
- Loosefrox: Gobspin Headlops = donut if glowing rings around the sword, else circle. Kinborrow (only while Popoto alive) = Popoto Skin Vulnerability Down 15s, removable. Gobbieboom Barrage = bombs; small bombs become large circles and can be pushed by attacking; a bomb pushed into a sand trap sinks harmlessly; large spiky bombs make a cross of lines. Bomb Toss = circle on player with knockback. Goblin Hammer = several circles. After Popoto dies: Heartyvow = permanent Damage Up; Goblin Rush = tankbuster.
- Popoto: Sand Breath = 90 degree cone from a sand trap, Blind 30s (cleanse); if a bomb is pushed into that trap Popoto takes damage, is stunned, Vulnerability Up (a damage window). Sand Pillar = small detonations between traps. After Loosefrox dies: Earthquake = multiple unavoidable hits.
- Priority: not stated by any source. (inf) Push bombs into Popoto's trap for the stun window; killing one buffs the other, so kill them close in time or kill Popoto first only if you can handle Heartyvow + Goblin Rush; Loosefrox first leaves Earthquake only.

# BOARD 3 (Lv50, rank 11, 12 moves, 14 roster) [B3 for layout/stats; attacks from SB (1, unofficial draft) unless noted]

Route: M1 Cavalier+Bone Bishop; M2 Ymir+Sahagin; M3 Shop #1; M4 split (L ELITE Zu+Cockerel+Pullet / R Catoblepas); M5 split (L Treasure #1 / R Campsite #1); M6 Lakhamu+Golem; M7 split (L Treasure #2 / R Shop #2); M8 ELITE Siren+Shambling+Crawling; M9 Treasure #3; M10 split (L random Campeador/Sabotender/Flowertender or Soldado/Guardia fight, or Treasure / R Campsite #2); M11 Shop #3; M12 Boss Guttler + Thanatos. Choose M4R then M5R to skip the elite and bank Campsite #1; M10R is Campsite #2 right before shop and boss. Rewards: Bright Remnant of Resilience x10 at Legendary. [B3] (1). Treasure and shop contents not listed.

## M1 Cavalier Piece + Bone Bishop
- Weak: Fire, Blunt. Cavalier vuln Poison [Cavalier_Piece page]; combined vulns Poison, Slow, Petrification, Paralysis, Stun, Bind [B3].
- Cavalier: Steelripper = 120 degree frontal cone, Damage Down 30s (page confirms front AoE, Slashing; SB gives angle) (2). Crushing Blade = tankbuster + targeted knockback. Doubling = absorbs up to 5 orbs from dead Bone Bishops, makes that many Feinted Cavalier copies.
- Feinted Cavalier: Menace = extremely large static circle on the copy; Valfodr = line from front, knockback.
- Bone Bishop: Blizzard auto; Soul Douse = self-destruct if alive too long (unavoidable, Damage Down).
- Priority: kill Bishops BEFORE Soul Douse and ideally before Cavalier casts Doubling, since their orbs fuel copies (SB, 1).

## M2 Ymir Piece + Sahagin Piece
- Weak: Ice, Blunt. Vuln Stun, Bind, Poison, Interruption, Blind [B3].
- Sahagin: Water II = three circles under you in sequence, Damage Down. Paralyzing Spikes = buff 40s, paralyzes attackers 20s (uncleansable); only while Ymir alive, removed when Ymir dies. Tsunami = directional knockback. Deadwash = INTERRUPT (else Hysteria + Damage Down).
- Ymir: Ymir Shell (destructible; Vulnerability Down while intact; crawls to another shell; with none left only Head Snatch). Head Snatch = frontal cleave (circle around the slow Ymir). Blanket Thunder = unavoidable, only while in shell.
- Priority (SB-derived, inf): kill Ymir to drop Sahagin's Paralyzing Spikes, or have beasts hit Sahagin. Interrupt Deadwash.

## M4L ELITE Zu + Cockerel + Pullet
- Zu weak: Earth; vuln Blind, Poison [Zu_Piece page]; set vulns include Sleep, Paralysis, Stun, Bind, Heavy, Interruption.
- Zu: Fore Carve / Rear Carve (Slashing, front/rear AoE) (page). Crossbreeze = cross of lines on player, can destroy eggs. Flying Frenzy = circle tankbuster, can destroy eggs. Rampaging Fore/Rear Carve = proximity tether, charge then 180 degree cone front/rear per cast name. Airy Pursuit = homing AoE, can destroy eggs. Brood Rage = each egg destroyed before hatching gives Zu an unremovable damage stack: keep AoEs off the eggs.
- Cockerel: Breakbeak auto. Pullet: Caustic Vomit = INTERRUPT (uncleansable Heavy 20s).

## M4R Catoblepas Piece
- Weak: Wind; vuln Poison.
- Bestial Roar = six Demonic Eyes (three Ring-Eyes -> donuts, three Orb-Eyes -> circles), Petrification 8s. Shifting Gaze = gaze on three eyes, Petrification 8s. Sinister Gleam = 180 degree cone. Jettatura = large circle under player. Both Vuln Up (SB).

## M6 Lakhamu Piece + Golem Piece
- Weak: Water; vuln Slow, Stun, Sleep, Flat damage.
- Lakhamu: Landslip = four alternating knockback lanes (paired with Golem Rockslide lines). Sand Tempest = unavoidable + Blind 15s, Sand Spheres burst into circles. Earthshaker = two spread lines (player, beast). Earthrender = three sequential circles.
- Golem: Rockslide = line from front (only with Landslip); Might = unremovable Damage Up 30s. Kill Golem first (inf) to stop Might/Rockslide; Lakhamu is the dodge-heavy one.

## M8 ELITE Siren + Shambling + Crawling
- Weak: Piercing, Fire; vuln Poison, Slow, Paralysis, Bind, Heavy.
- Siren: Song of Torment = tankbuster. Unmooring Melody = cone from random edge, stacks Wit's End. Feral Lunge = large line from random edge, knockback, Damage Down. Dead Man's Dirge = wings covering -> large circle; otherwise donut with small safe zone; +3 Wit's End. Distant Tune = three sets of Sweet Song bubbles bursting in order. Inviting Verse = random Forced March 16s. Summon = Shamblers and Crawlers.
- Shambler: Wallop small line. Crawler: Death Throes = Bind + Damage Down if it reaches you; kill it before it reaches you.
- Wit's End stacks likely confuse at threshold as on Board 2 (inf).

## M10L random: Campeador/Sabotender/Flowertender or Soldado/Guardia
- Weak: Lightning (Soldado/Guardia also Ice).
- Campeador: Seeding Needles = unavoidable while Guardia lives; after Guardia dies, foot stamp -> large circle, hover -> donut. Needle Gnarl = circle on player. Guardia: Cactguard redirects damage to itself (kill Guardia first). Flowertender: Healing Water heals Sabotenders (kill Flowertender first, inf). Soldado: four get number markers and place large circles in order.

## M12 BOSS: Guttler the Gutter ("Breaker of Beasts", Behemoth) + Thanatos Piece
- Guttler Lv50, weak none, vuln Poison (page); combined vulns Poison, Slow, Petrification, Stun [B3]. All stats 5 stars. Thanatos weak Lightning; vuln Slow, Petrification, Stun; Infernal Pain universal unaspected (page).
- Gyrocleave = massive line over nearly whole arena; only small squares E and W are safe. Beastly Aura = knockback N and S from center (page: Wind, universal). Combusting Blades = random-position circles, always combined with Gluttonous Goring (huge circle on Guttler, hover + glowing axe) or Gluttonous Gutting (huge line in front, grounded glowing swing). Thunderbolt = line tankbuster, uncleansable Paralysis 15s (page). Overpowering Point = line on player, Confused 12s; if aimed at the suits of armor on E/W walls it spawns Thanatos (B3 dialogue hint + SB) (2). Deadly Demesne = chained enclosures with green daggers: entering gives Bind+Fetter; daggers fire lines (Damage Down, Poison 24s cleansable). Molten Metal = circle under player, flaming dagger then proximity AoE.
- Thanatos: Infernal Pain self-destruct if left alive (unavoidable, Vuln Up); kill it fast, so keep Overpowering Point away from the armors.
- Lauda dialogue: Guttler may assume her form, illusions; show no quarter to the shade he fashions (web snippet, B3 page) (1).
